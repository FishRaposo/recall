# Darters are undodgeable

*Design review notes from a playtest session*

> Watched four people play this afternoon. Every one of them died to darters on wave 2 or 3, and every one said the same thing: "I moved and it still got me." Watching over their shoulders, they're doing the right thing: they see the red line, they sidestep. The line follows them. Then the darter lunges at where they are now, not where the line was when it showed up.
>
> The telegraph is the whole contract with this enemy. When the line appears, the darter has committed: that's the direction it lunges, and the player's job is to get off the line. Right now the line is a lie. Please make darters honor it.
