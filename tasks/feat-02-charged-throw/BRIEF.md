# Charged throw

*Producer request*

> Players are asking for a "big throw" for clearing packs, and we want a skill expression that isn't just faster clicking. Let's do hold-to-charge on the throw button. Tap stays exactly as it is today: nobody who never holds should notice any change. Hold long enough and you get a throw that goes about twice as far and doesn't get slowed down by brutes. The cost: you move slowly while charging, so charging in the middle of a swarm is a real decision. Needs to be readable at a glance: players should see when they're charging and when it's ready. Works on mouse, keyboard and controller.

**Numbers from design**

| | |
|---|---|
| Full charge | 0.35 s held |
| Charged throw speed | 1.4 × normal (travel ≈ 2×, since distance scales with speed²) |
| Movement while charging | 55 % of normal speed |
| Released before full charge | a normal throw |
